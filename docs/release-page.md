Real-time sound system and room acoustics analyzer: RTA, transfer function, impulse response, room acoustics, EQ assistant, SPL meter and remote access from phones and tablets.

**Download:** Windows `…-win-x64.exe` (installer) or `…-portable.exe` · macOS `…-mac-arm64.dmg` (Apple Silicon) or `…-mac-x64.dmg` (Intel) · Linux `…-linux-x86_64.AppImage` or `…-linux-arm64.AppImage`

Not code-signed yet: on Windows choose **More info → Run anyway**; on macOS right-click the app → **Open** the first time.

**New in this version**

- Saved spectrum traces keep their own colour (they were tinted and hidden by the live fill).
- A thorough check of the whole app: dozens of fixes, safer handling of files and remote devices, and faster graphs.
- Separate input and output devices, native Core Audio and JACK / PipeWire / ALSA, loudness meter (LUFS), distortion from sweeps, air absorption compensation and the EQ board.
- Allen & Heath EQ widths that match the console, and the dLive NEQ12.
